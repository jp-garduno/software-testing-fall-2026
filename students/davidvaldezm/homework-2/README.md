## Quality Goals

- Never silently lose, duplicate, or alter a financial transaction.
- Prevent unauthorized access and avoid exposing sensitive data.
- Keep balances, budgets, and reports mathematically consistent.
- Make critical flows accessible and understandable on supported devices.
- Detect high-impact regressions before release and monitor residual risk in production.
