## Application: Instagram - Social Media Platform

**Purpose**: 
Instagram is a highly popular social media platform designed for sharing photos, videos, and short-form content (Reels/Stories). It allows users to connect with friends, discover new content through a personalized feed, and interact via likes, comments, and direct messages. The platform also serves as a critical marketing and e-commerce tool for businesses and influencers.

**Target Users**:
- **General Consumers**: Individuals looking to share moments, follow friends, and consume entertaining content.
- **Content Creators & Influencers**: Users focused on building an audience, personal branding, and monetization.
- **Businesses and Advertisers**: Brands looking to market products, run targeted ad campaigns, and engage with customers directly.

**Key Features**:
1. User registration, authentication, and profile management.
2. Content creation and publishing (Photos, Videos, Stories, and Reels) with various filters and editing tools.
3. Interactive algorithmic Feed and Explore page.
4. Direct Messaging (DMs) including text, voice notes, and media sharing.
5. Social interactions (Likes, comments, shares, saves, and tagging).
6. E-commerce integration (Instagram Shopping) and ad placements.

**Technology Stack**:
- **Frontend**: Mobile Applications (iOS and Android built natively and with React Native), Web (React.js).
- **Backend**: Python (Django framework), Node.js for specific microservices.
- **Database & Storage**: PostgreSQL, Cassandra, Memcached, Redis, and AWS/Facebook proprietary CDN infrastructure for media storage.

**Critical Functions**:
The mission-critical functions of Instagram include user authentication, the ability to successfully upload and view media (photos/videos), the core feed algorithm to keep users engaged, and the direct messaging system. If a user cannot log in or cannot view/post pictures, the application loses its primary value entirely. Furthermore, for the business side, the ad delivery network and shopping functionalities are critical for revenue generation.
