About

I am a fullstack developer from Guadalajara, Mexico. I started as an intern in 2021 and have spent the last five years building web and mobile products, mostly in TypeScript and mostly internal business tooling: payroll, ticketing, reporting, client management.

Along the way I have done more than write code. At Yapsi I stepped into a project manager role during a rough stretch of missed deadlines, restructured planning for two teams under Scrum, and got both projects delivered on time.

I care about best practices and code other people can reuse, and I would rather learn from the people around me than guess. Right now I am filling in the half of the craft I have practiced least: proving that what I build actually works.
